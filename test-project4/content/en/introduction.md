Every mission starts the same way: a console lighting up, a channel opening, and someone on the other end who needs to know exactly what's running and why. That's what this manual is — the operations manual for your own mission control.

APOGEE doesn't hide telemetry behind a dashboard you can't reconfigure. Every reading is yours to see, every system yours to calibrate, every panel yours to rewire before launch.

## Why a flight manual, not a feature list

Most tools hand you a feature list and call it documentation. A feature list tells you what exists. A flight manual tells you what to do when it matters — what to check before ignition, what a status light means, what to do when telemetry goes quiet.

APOGEE is built the second way. Every page assumes you're about to fly something, not browse a catalog.

{% callout type="note" title="Telemetry" %}
Everything past this page assumes your console is powered and your channel is open. If it isn't — go back and finish preflight. Nothing here works with a dead console.
{% /callout %}

Here's what the rest of this manual covers.

### Reading the instruments

Every panel — status lights, console modules, the mission display itself — reports exactly what it's doing and nothing it isn't. If a light is red, something needs your attention. If it's green, you're cleared to proceed.

### Calibrating your own systems

Livery, cockpit display, comm channels — every system ships with its own calibration file. Nothing is factory-locked. If you can see a dial, you can turn it.

### Clearing the pad

Generate a static build and stage it on any launchpad you already control. No mission control back-office required, no clearance to request from anyone but yourself.

### Standing down cleanly

A mission isn't finished until the debrief. This manual ends with what most flight manuals skip: how to hand off cleanly, log the flight, and leave the pad clear for the next launch.

Systems nominal? Let's begin the sequence.
